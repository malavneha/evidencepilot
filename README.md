

# EvidencePilot

## An autonomous AI research agent for evidence-focused clinical research

Autonomous AI research agent for evidence-backed clinical research workflows.

EvidencePilot is designed to help researchers turn complex clinical research questions into structured, evidence-focused research briefs.

Instead of acting as a simple question-and-answer chatbot, EvidencePilot is designed as a multi-step agentic workflow that can plan research tasks, gather relevant evidence, organize findings, identify evidence gaps, and synthesize results.

## Problem

Clinical research often requires researchers to search across multiple sources, review large amounts of information, compare findings, and identify unanswered questions.

This can be repetitive and time-consuming.

EvidencePilot explores how an autonomous AI agent can reduce this repetitive workload while keeping the researcher in control.

## Planned Workflow

1. Receive a complex clinical research question.
2. Break the question into smaller research tasks.
3. Identify relevant evidence.
4. Extract and organize important findings.
5. Evaluate the collected evidence.
6. Identify evidence gaps and uncertainty.
7. Synthesize the findings.
8. Produce a structured research brief with traceable sources.

## Design Principles

- Evidence should remain traceable to its sources.
- AI-generated synthesis should be distinguishable from retrieved evidence.
- Uncertainty and limitations should be visible.
- The system should assist researchers rather than replace clinical judgment.
- The workflow should demonstrate genuine agentic task execution.
